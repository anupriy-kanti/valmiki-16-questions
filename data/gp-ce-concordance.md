# Gita Press (vulgate) to Critical Edition, Bālakāṇḍa sarga 1, verses 1 to 21

Made by mytwiki-C21, 5 Nov 2026, by matching the artefact's GP half-lines against the GRETIL CE text. Read by Claude, not yet checked by Anupriy on the page.

- GP 1–12 = CE 1.1.1–12 (same numbers).
- GP 13: first half *prajāpatisamaḥ śrīmān dhātā ripuniṣūdanaḥ* **not in the CE**. Second half *rakṣitā jīvalokasya dharmasya parirakṣitā* = CE 1.1.13a.
- GP 14: first half *rakṣitā svasya dharmasya svajanasya ca rakṣitā* **not in the CE**. Second half *vedavedāṅgatattvajño dhanurvede ca niṣṭhitaḥ* = CE 1.1.13b.
- GP 15 = CE 1.1.14
- GP 16 = CE 1.1.15
- GP 17 = CE 1.1.16
- GP 18 = CE 1.1.17
- GP 19 = CE 1.1.18
- GP 20: first half *jyeṣṭhaṃ jyeṣṭhaguṇair yuktaṃ priyaṃ daśarathaḥ sutam* = CE 1.1.19a (CE reads *śreṣṭhaguṇair*). Second half *prakṛtīnāṃ hitair yuktaṃ prakṛtipriyakāmyayā* **not in the CE**.
- GP 21: first half *yauvarājyena saṃyoktum aicchat prītyā mahīpatiḥ* = CE 1.1.19b. Second half (*tasyābhiṣekasambhārān dṛṣṭvā bhāryātha kaikayī*) is the next stretch of story, beyond this site.

**Moving the English:** Debroy (a CE translation) strings attach to the CE verse. For GP 13 and 14 the artefact's Debroy text ("He is a protector of the world of the living…", "He knows the truth about the Vedas…") belongs to CE 1.1.13. Gita Press English for a split verse goes with the CE verse holding the matching half, and the vulgate-only part sits in the GP-reading box.

"Not in the CE" here means **not in the constituted text**. Whether these half-lines are in the CE's apparatus or appendix is unchecked, so say "not in the constituted text", never "excised" or "interpolated".
